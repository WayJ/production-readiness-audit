# Community conduct / 社区行为准则

Be respectful and specific. Critique code and evidence, not people. Assume good intent while taking safety concerns seriously. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse and publishing private information are not welcome.

请尊重他人，围绕实现与证据讨论，不攻击个人。不接受骚扰、威胁、歧视性言论和公开隐私。讨论真实软件问题时保护客户与贡献者信息。

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or limit participation proportionately. Report misconduct through a private maintainer channel where available; do not repost harmful private material in public. Security disclosures follow SECURITY.md.

These project-specific guidelines apply to the repository's issues, reviews and discussions. They are not a promise of continuous moderation or a response-time guarantee.
